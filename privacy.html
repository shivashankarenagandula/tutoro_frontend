Privacy Notice

Last updated: version v1

Tutoro ("we", "us") connects parents in Hyderabad with tutors and partner academies, and coordinates every step of that match. This notice explains what personal data we collect through this website and our forms, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023).

What we collect

  • Name, phone number, and (optionally) email address
  • Your area/locality, and for parents, your child's class/grade and the subject(s) needed
  • For tutors: subjects taught, experience, and expected fee
  • Any free-text notes you choose to add about your requirements

Why we collect it

Solely to match you with a suitable tutor, academy, or student, and to contact you about that match — by phone, WhatsApp, or email. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Who sees it

Your contact details are visible to the Tutoro team coordinating your match. Tutoro stays the intermediary throughout — your phone number is not shared directly with a tutor or parent without your and their agreement, and Tutoro coordinates the introduction.

How long we keep it

We retain your data for as long as needed to provide the matching service, and for a reasonable period afterward for support and record- keeping. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To do any of these, contact us using the details below.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, reach us at +91 9347696298 or via the WhatsApp link on our homepage.

This notice is provided as a good-faith summary of our data practices and is not a substitute for formal legal advice. If you're relying on this page for a live product handling real user data at scale, we'd recommend having it reviewed by a qualified professional familiar with the DPDP Act.
