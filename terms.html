What Tutoro does

Tutoro is a matching and coordination service connecting parents in Hyderabad with independent tutors and partner academies. Tutoro is not the employer of any tutor, and does not itself provide tutoring services — tutors are independent individuals, and academies are independent institutions Tutoro has a referral relationship with.

Free demo classes

Requesting a demo class through Tutoro does not obligate a parent to continue with a tutor after the demo, and does not obligate a tutor to continue teaching a student they were matched with. Either side may decide not to proceed after the demo.

Fees

Tutors set their own fees for ongoing classes, shown as either an online rate or a home-visit rate depending on the mode of teaching. Requesting a demo class is free. Any commission or referral arrangement with a partner academy is between Tutoro and that academy, and does not change the fee a family pays the academy directly.

Staff-mediated contact

Tutoro stays the coordinator throughout a match — introductions, scheduling, and any changes are handled through Tutoro's team rather than by sharing contact details directly between a parent and tutor without both sides' agreement.

Conduct

Both parents and tutors agree to communicate respectfully and honestly through the platform, and to provide accurate information when submitting a request or a tutor profile.
